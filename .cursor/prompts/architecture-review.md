# Architecture Review Prompt

Review architecture as a **cloud-native staff engineer**.

## Dimensions

- Modularity & coupling
- Scalability & bottlenecks (parse fan-out, Neo4j hotspots)
- Reliability patterns (retries, idempotency, backpressure)
- Security posture & tenant isolation
- Data lifecycle & reproducibility
- Observability & operability
- Cost model drivers (tokens, graph size, storage)

## Output

**Strengths**, **risks**, **recommended changes** (prioritized), **tradeoffs** explicitly stated.

Ensure alignment with deterministic-first, graph-centric, explainable AI orchestration.
